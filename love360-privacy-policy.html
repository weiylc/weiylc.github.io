This Privacy Policy explains how Good APP Studio ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Love360 mobile application (the "App"). It applies to the Love360 application published by Good APP Studio on Google Play and the Apple App Store.

Important: precise and background location

Love360 collects precise location data to enable continuous real-time location sharing with your paired partner and location route history, even when the App is closed, not in use, or the device screen is locked.

When you enable Location Sharing and grant the relevant permissions, your precise location, location timestamp, accuracy, movement speed, and device battery level are transmitted to our servers. Your latest location and location history are made available to the partner account that you have deliberately paired with in Love360.

Background location is optional and requires a separate in-app disclosure and permission. If you decline background location, you can continue to use the map and location features while the App is in the foreground. You can stop new location collection and sharing by turning off Location Sharing in Love360 Settings. You can also revoke location permission in your device settings or unpair your partner.

We do not sell precise location data, and we do not provide Love360 precise GPS location to advertising providers for advertising.

1. Information we collect and process

1.1 Account and profile information

  • Google or Apple sign-in identifier and authentication token;
  • name, email address, nickname, profile photo, and Love360 user identifier;
  • invite code, paired-partner identifier, preferred language, and account settings.

Your Google or Apple password is not provided to Love360.

1.2 Location information

  • precise latitude and longitude;
  • location timestamp, accuracy, and movement speed;
  • latest location and route history;
  • location permission and Location Sharing status.

Love360 collects background location only after the required disclosure, your affirmative action, and the applicable device permission.

1.3 Partner communications and safety features

  • pairing relationship and invite-code activity;
  • chat messages and message metadata;
  • SOS alerts and related sender, recipient, and time information;
  • information you choose to share with your paired partner through Love360 features.

1.4 Optional device activity information

If you enable the relevant feature and grant the required device or special-access permission, Love360 may process and share with your paired partner:

  • battery level and charging events;
  • Wi-Fi network name and network-change events;
  • device unlock count and App-open count;
  • screen-time summary, foreground application name, and usage duration;
  • changes to selected system permission states.

These features can be controlled through Love360 notification or permission settings and through your device settings.

1.5 Purchases, advertising, analytics, and technical information

  • subscription entitlement, product, transaction, and expiration information;
  • push notification token and notification preferences;
  • device model, operating system, App version, language, IP address, and network diagnostics;
  • advertising identifier, ad impressions, ad interactions, and consent choices;
  • App interaction, performance, crash, and diagnostic events.

2. How we use information

We use information to:

  • authenticate users and maintain Love360 accounts;
  • provide pairing, continuous partner location sharing, maps, and route history;
  • deliver chat, SOS, device-event, and push-notification features;
  • provide subscriptions, restore purchases, and determine feature access;
  • display and measure advertising in the free version of the App;
  • maintain security, prevent abuse, diagnose errors, and improve performance;
  • respond to support, privacy, and legal requests.

3. How information is shared

3.1 Your paired partner

When you pair with another Love360 account, the information used by partner features may be visible to that paired partner. Depending on the features you enable, this includes your latest location, route history, battery level, profile information, messages, SOS alerts, and optional device activity events. Unpairing stops future partner access through the pairing relationship but does not automatically erase information already delivered or retained as described below.

3.2 Service providers

We use service providers to operate Love360. They process information on our behalf or under their own terms as necessary to provide their services:

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS): API hosting, authentication backend, databases, file storage, real-time communication, and push delivery infrastructure;
  • Google: Google Sign-In, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Google Maps, Google Play services, and Google AdMob;
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple and Apple platform services;
  • RevenueCat: subscription entitlement and purchase management;
  • Umeng: App analytics and performance measurement.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the public, or in connection with a corporate reorganization subject to appropriate safeguards.

We do not sell or rent personal information.

4. Advertising

The free version of Love360 displays ads through Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may process advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, approximate location inferred from IP address, consent choices, and ad interaction information under Google's terms and your device privacy choices. Love360 does not provide your precise GPS location or private partner content to AdMob for advertising. Advertising may be disabled for users with an eligible paid subscription.

5. Data storage and retention

Love360 data is stored locally on your device and on service-provider infrastructure, including AWS infrastructure. Data may be processed in Hong Kong and other countries where we or our providers operate.

  • Location route history: retained for up to 30 days;
  • SOS records: retained for up to 7 days;
  • chat and partner activity messages: retained for up to 90 days;
  • profile, pairing, latest-location, push-token, and subscription status: retained while needed to operate your account or until deletion, unless a longer period is required for security, fraud prevention, accounting, or law;
  • local preferences and cached data: retained until cleared, deleted in the App where available, or removed by uninstalling the App;
  • provider records: retained according to the applicable provider's terms and legal obligations.

6. Your controls and choices

  • Turn off Location Sharing in Love360 Settings to stop new location collection and partner sharing by Love360;
  • revoke foreground or background location permission in device settings;
  • unpair your partner to end the Love360 pairing relationship;
  • manage notification and optional device-feature permissions in Love360 and device settings;
  • reset your advertising identifier or change advertising privacy choices in device settings;
  • sign out or stop using the App.

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information controlled by Good APP Studio by emailing devapptian@gmail.com. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information, including authenticated API access and encrypted HTTPS/WSS transmission. No electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's privacy

Love360 is not directed to children under 13, or under the higher minimum age required to consent to data processing in their country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information improperly.
