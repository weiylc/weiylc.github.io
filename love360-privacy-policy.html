Love360 Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Good APP Studio ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Love360 mobile application (the "App"). Love360 is published on Google Play by Good APP Studio.

Important notice about background location

Love360 collects precise location data to enable continuous location sharing and location history even when the App is closed, not in use, or the device screen is locked.

When Location Sharing is enabled, this location data is transmitted securely to our servers, stored as location history, and shared with the partner you pair with in Love360 so that the partner can view your current location and route history. We do not use precise or background location data to provide advertising.

Background location is optional. You can decline background access and continue using map and location features while the App is in the foreground. You can stop collection and sharing by turning off Location Sharing in the App's Settings, revoke location permission in Android Settings, or unpair your partner.

3. Permissions and your choices

  • Foreground location: used for the map and location features while you are using the App.
  • Background location: requested separately on Android for continuous location sharing and location history when the App is closed or not in use. It is optional.
  • Notifications: used for chat, SOS, partner events, and service notifications. You can disable them in device settings.
  • Photos/files: used only when you choose an image to upload or send.
  • Usage Access: an optional Android special-access permission used for screen-time and app-usage insights. You can revoke it in Android Settings.

Turning off Location Sharing in Love360 Settings stops Love360's location collection and sharing process. You can also revoke foreground or background location access at any time through Android Settings > Apps > Love360 > Permissions > Location. Unpairing stops sharing data with the former partner.
