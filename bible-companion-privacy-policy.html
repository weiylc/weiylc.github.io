Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Bible Companion (the “App”), provided by Good APP Studio (“we,” “us,” or “our”), handles information when you use the App. We respect your privacy and aim to explain our practices clearly.

1. Information You May Provide

Depending on the features you choose to use, Bible Companion may process information such as reading preferences, bookmarks, highlighted passages, notes, prayer entries, devotional reflections, search queries, and other content you enter. Some information may be stored locally on your device. If account, backup, or synchronization features are available and enabled, information may also be processed by the relevant service providers.

2. AI and Online Features

If you choose to use AI-assisted Bible study, devotional guidance, or other online features, the text you submit and relevant context may be transmitted to service providers to generate a response. Avoid entering sensitive personal information into these features. AI-generated responses may be inaccurate and should not replace your own judgment.

3. Device and Technical Information

Depending on the App's configuration and integrated services, technical information such as device type, operating system, app version, diagnostic events, crash information, and general usage metrics may be processed to operate, secure, and improve the App.

4. Advertising and Analytics

If the App includes advertising or analytics services, their providers may process identifiers and device or interaction data in accordance with their own privacy policies and your device permissions and privacy settings. The specific services and data collected depend on the version and configuration of the App.

5. Purchases and Subscriptions

If purchases or subscriptions are offered, transactions are generally handled by the applicable app store or payment platform. We do not receive your full payment card details from the app store. We may receive transaction or entitlement information needed to provide purchased features.

6. How Information Is Used

  • To provide Bible reading, study, search, notes, bookmarks, and devotional features.
  • To support optional online or AI-powered functions you request.
  • To maintain performance, troubleshoot errors, and protect the App.
  • To manage purchases and subscriptions where applicable.

7. Sharing and Disclosure

Information may be shared with service providers only as needed to deliver enabled features, such as cloud processing, AI responses, analytics, advertising, or purchase verification. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect users and our services. We do not claim that personal devotional or prayer content is never transmitted; the treatment of such content depends on the features you use.

8. Retention and Your Choices

You may be able to edit or delete locally saved reading history, bookmarks, notes, and prayer entries using the App's controls. Removing the App generally removes its local data, subject to device backups. Data processed by online providers may be retained under their respective policies. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact us using the email below.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

Bible Companion is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

11. Third-Party Services and Links

The App may link to external Bible resources or use third-party technologies. Those services have their own privacy practices, which we encourage you to review.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the App, service providers, or legal requirements. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
